About GRIPS2

The Next Evolution in Industrial Energy

GRIPS2 (Grid Resilience through Intelligent Photovoltaics and Storage - Phase 2) is scaling up a successful Phase 1 pilot to implement Nepal's first industrial-scale smart solar and battery microgrid at the Laxmi Steel factory in Sunwal.

Our innovative solution combines solar PV, battery storage, and AI-driven control systems to enhance energy reliability, reduce costs, and support decarbonization of Nepal's industrial sector.

As a "lighthouse" project funded by the UNIDO A2D Facility, GRIPS2 demonstrates how clean energy technologies can transform industrial operations in developing countries, providing a model that can be replicated across Nepal and beyond.

Challenge & Solution

1The Challenge

Nepal's industrial sector faces critical energy challenges that hamper growth and competitiveness:

  • Unreliable Grid Power: Frequent outages and voltage fluctuations causing production disruptions and equipment damage
  • Expensive Diesel Backup: High costs for diesel generators that eat into profits
  • Carbon Footprint: Increasing pressure to decarbonize as global markets implement regulations like CBAM
  • Investment Barriers: Hesitation to be "first movers" in adopting new clean energy technologies

2Our Solution

GRIPS2 addresses these challenges through an integrated approach:

  • Smart Microgrid: 1MW solar PV system with battery storage providing reliable power during outages
  • AI-Driven Control: Swanbarton's Microgrid Management System (SB-MMS) optimizing energy use and storage
  • Cost Efficiency: Reducing dependence on diesel and minimizing grid electricity costs
  • De-risked Model: Proving the business case to catalyze broader adoption across Nepal's industrial sector

Technical Approach

☀️

Solar PV System

1MW capacity solar photovoltaic system strategically installed to maximize energy generation while utilizing available factory roof space, generating clean energy directly where it's needed.

🔋

Battery Energy Storage

Advanced battery system providing critical backup power during outages, enabling energy time-shifting to reduce peak charges, and stabilizing voltage fluctuations to protect sensitive equipment.

🧠

Intelligent Control

Swanbarton's Microgrid Management System uses AI algorithms to predict load patterns, integrate weather data, and optimize energy flows, ensuring maximum cost savings and system performance.

Key Benefits

💰

Economic Advantage

Reduce energy costs by up to 32% through decreased diesel consumption and optimized grid usage, with projected savings of $1.3 million over 25 years.

⚡

Enhanced Reliability

Achieve 99.9% power reliability, eliminating production disruptions and improving product quality through stabilized power supply.

🌍

Carbon Reduction

Offset 2,800 tonnes of CO₂ emissions, supporting Nepal's NDC targets and preparing industries for carbon border regulations.

👥

Social Impact

Improve working conditions for 500+ factory workers, create new technical jobs, and support gender equality through inclusive training programs.
